FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email me and I'll reply directly.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?+

SEO helps your pages rank in traditional search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures content so it's chosen as the direct answer in featured snippets, AI Overviews and voice results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes your brand easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude to understand, trust and cite.

What does an audit include?+

A technical and on-page review of your priority pages, an LLM citation gap analysis showing where AI assistants cite competitors instead of you, schema and entity checks, LLM readability recommendations, and a prioritized action plan.

Which industries do you work with?+

SaaS, education, professional services and local service businesses in the US, UK and India.

Can you work across US and UK time zones?+

Yes. I've managed SEO campaigns remotely for US and UK clients since 2020.
